# Cookies

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Zotune uses no advertising cookies. It keeps a few things in your browser so it can work offline and remember your choices.

## Kept by zotune

- **The dictionary, bookmarks, likes, answers and suggestions**, in the browser's database (IndexedDB), named `zotune`.
- **The app itself**, in the browser's cache, so it opens without a connection.
- **Your theme, your choice about usage statistics and your recent words**, in the browser's local storage (`zotune.theme`, `zotune.analytics`, `zotune.recent`).

These never leave your device on their own. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes them.

## Google Analytics

Only after you allow usage statistics, Google Analytics sets the cookies `_ga` and `_ga_…` to tell visits apart. They are removed when you turn statistics off in [Settings](/settings#privacy). See [Privacy](/privacy#usage-statistics) for what is counted.

## Cloudflare

Cloudflare, which serves the site, may set a short-lived security cookie (such as `__cf_bm`) to tell people from automated traffic.
