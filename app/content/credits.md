# Credits

Zotune stands on the work of many people: those who wrote Zolai down, those who collected its words, and those who check each meaning today. This page names them.

## The dictionary

The dictionary is kept in the [laisiangtho/bible](https://github.com/laisiangtho/bible) project by Khen Solomon Lethil. Every meaning starts as a draft and becomes reviewed or confirmed when Zolai speakers check it. Thank you to everyone who answers "Is this meaning correct?" and suggests a better one.

## Bible

Example sentences with a verse reference, such as *Late 45:15*, are quoted from the Zolai (Tedim) Bible.
