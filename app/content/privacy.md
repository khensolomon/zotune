# Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Zotune is a Zolai–English dictionary. There is no account to create, and the dictionary works without sending anything about you.

## On your device

Bookmarks, likes, your helpful and correct answers, suggestions and recent words are kept in your browser. They stay there until you clear the site's data. The dictionary itself is downloaded once and kept in your browser, so it works offline.

## What is downloaded

The dictionary tables come from the [laisiangtho/bible](https://github.com/laisiangtho/bible) repository on GitHub. Your browser downloads them from `raw.githubusercontent.com`, so GitHub sees the request, as it does for any file it serves.

## Answers and suggestions

When sending is switched on, only your answers and suggestions themselves are sent, so reviewers can check them. Until then they wait on your device; Settings shows what is waiting.

## Usage statistics

Usage statistics are collected only if you allow them. They are counted by Google Analytics and help show which words people look for and which are missing. What is counted:

- the pages you open, except that results which are not dictionary words are counted under a general address, without the text you typed;
- what you search for: a single word and whether it was found; phrases and sentences are counted without their text;
- when you bookmark, like or answer.

Advertising features are off. Google receives your IP address as part of each request. You can change your choice at any time in [Settings](/settings#privacy). If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, statistics stay off. In the desktop app they are never collected.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare processes each request to deliver the page and to protect the site from abuse.
