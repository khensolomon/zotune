# Terms

Last updated 17 October 2026.

## Using zotune

Zotune is free to use. Entries start as drafts and become reviewed or confirmed when Zolai speakers check them, so a meaning may still be incomplete or wrong. Please treat drafts with care.

## The dictionary

The dictionary data comes from the [laisiangtho/bible](https://github.com/laisiangtho/bible) project and is used under its licence.

## Your suggestions

Suggestions and answers you send are shared with the community under the same licence as the dictionary data. A reviewer checks every suggestion before it is published.

## The app

The source code of zotune is published under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/khensolomon/zotune/blob/master/LICENSE). The app is provided as it is, without warranty.
